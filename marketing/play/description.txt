Hexstead is a cozy board game of dice, land, and rivalry. Settle a valley of hexes, and claim the realm before the seasons turn.

Every round starts with a roll of the dice - every hex matching the roll pays its owner. Spend your harvest to claim new tiles, upgrade your land, and chase your secret goal. But watch the roads: roll a seven and the bandit rides, blocking a hex and stealing from whoever owns it.

 HOW IT PLAYS
 - Roll two dice, then choose: activate the sum, or each die on its own
 - Claim free tiles with wood and brick, upgrade them to double their yield
 - Play cards to bend the rules: shift a die, charter distant land, or send the bandit after a rival
 - Buy landmarks for lasting powers, like warding the bandit off your fields
 - First to 15 points wins - or the richest realm when round 15 ends

 SHARP RIVALS
 Face 1 to 3 bots with real strategies of their own. Start Easy, then test yourself against Fair and Cruel opponents with bigger score bonuses on the line.

 MADE FOR QUICK SESSIONS
 A full match runs about ten minutes. Play offline, anywhere, in portrait with one hand.
